Data & privacy

The short version: the game collects nothing about you, and nobody here can see your valley.

Last revised 4 September 2026

What the game stores

Your saved lives — the valley, the camp, the people, the chronicle — are written to your own device. That is where they live. Nothing about how you play is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent: the game has no server of its own, no account to sign into, and no analytics of any kind built into it.

No advertising. No trackers. No third-party analytics or attribution kits. No profile of you is built, sold, or shared.

What leaves the device, and where it goes

Three Apple services can carry data off your device, and only if you have them switched on:

  • Game Center keeps a copy of your saves so a life started on the iPhone can be continued on the iPad or in the headset. Apple holds that copy under your Apple Account, not ours. Sign out of Game Center and the game plays exactly the same, on that device alone.
  • iCloud stores a small amount of settings and progress in the same way, again under your own account.
  • The App Store handles any purchase. We never see a card number, an address or a name — Apple tells us only that a purchase happened.

Each of those is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and each can be turned off in your device's own settings.

The rating prompt

At most once per version, after a life has run thirty days and raised something, the game may ask what you think of it. That prompt is Apple's, it is shown by the system, and whatever you tap is not reported back to us in any form that identifies you.

Children

The game asks for no personal information from anyone, of any age, and has no chat, no social features and no user-to-user contact.

This website

The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps ordinary server logs — the address that made a request, the page asked for, the time. We set no cookies and run no analytics script; there is nothing here to consent to.

If you write to us using the form, what you send us is exactly what you type: your note, and a name and an email address if you choose to give them. We use them to answer you and to keep a record of what readers have asked for. They are not sold, not shared, and not added to any marketing list. Ask us and we will delete your message and your address.

How long anything is kept

Saved games last until you delete them or delete the app. Letters sent through the form are kept while they are still useful and deleted on request. Server logs age out on the host's own schedule.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it, and the change will be described in the game's own "What's new".

Asking us anything

Write through the form on the main page and it reaches a person.
